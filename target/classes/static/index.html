Bridging the gap between donors and those in need

Find My Blood is a digital platform designed to connect blood donors with people who urgently need blood. By allowing users to search for available donors based on blood group and location, Find My Blood helps make the process of finding blood faster, simpler, and more accessible.

Our goal is to reduce the time and effort involved in finding a suitable blood donor. Whether you are looking for blood for a loved one or willing to donate, Find My Blood provides a simple way to connect people when every moment matters.

What Find My Blood does

Blood donation

Register once and get notified only when your blood group is needed nearby. Track your donation history over time.

Find blood

Search active donors by blood group and city when a family member or patient needs blood urgently.

Donor registration

A two-minute sign-up puts you on call for your city's hospitals. Update or pause your availability anytime.

Emergency help

Post a critical request and reach every matching donor in your area within minutes, not hours.
